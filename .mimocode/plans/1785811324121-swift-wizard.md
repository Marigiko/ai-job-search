- **Stack:** Node.js/NestJS, TypeScript, Python (FastAPI/Django), React/Next.js, AWS, Docker/K8s, AI/LLM

## Market Comparison

### 1. Brasil ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (BEST CHANCE)

**Why it's the best fit:**
- **Portuguese B2** — Can communicate effectively, understand job postings, do interviews
- **Same timezone** (UTC-3) as Argentina — no schedule friction
- **No visa needed** — Mercosur agreement allows Argentines to live and work in Brasil
- **Large tech market** — São Paulo, Florianópolis, Rio have thriving tech scenes
- **Senior salaries:** R$15-25K/mes (~$3,000-5,000 USD) for seniors — 3-5x the $1000 floor
- **Remote-friendly** — Many companies hire remote within Brasil

**Evidence from our search:**
- 179+ senior desenvolvedor positions on freehire
- G4F, BRQ, FCamara, EPAM, Stefanini are strong matches
- All roles posted within last 7 days (fresh)

**Risk level:** LOW — no visa barrier, language fits, timezone matches

---

### 2. EU ⭐⭐⭐⭐ (STRONG SECOND)

**Why it's a strong option:**
- **High salaries** — €60-100K/year for seniors (~$65-110K USD)
- **Strong demand** — EU has shortage of senior engineers
- **Visa pathways** — Germany Blue Card, Netherlands Orientation Year, Portugal Tech Visa
- **English often sufficient** — Many tech companies operate in English
- **Good work-life balance**

**Evidence from our search:**
- Refurbed (Austria, sustainable e-commerce, EU-wide remote)
- Text (Poland, AI company)
- Moniepoint (Poland, fintech)
- Connectis (Poland, DevOps)

**Risk level:** MEDIUM — visa takes 2-4 months, timezone 4-6 hours ahead, some countries require local language

---

### 3. LATAM (ex-Brasil) ⭐⭐⭐ (GOOD OPTION)

**Why it's viable:**
- **Same timezone** as Argentina
- **No visa issues** — Most LATAM countries allow Argentine residents
- **Growing remote market** — Colombia, Mexico, Chile have strong tech scenes
- **English-friendly** — Many multinational companies with LATAM offices

**Evidence from our search:**
- AgileEngine (Colombia, Aug 4)
- Abstra (Argentina-based, LATAM-wide, Aug 3) — Mario already applied
- truelogic (LATAM)

**Risk level:** LOW-MEDIUM — Lower salaries than US/EU but good for entry

---

### 4. USA ⭐⭐ (HARDEST)

**Why it's the hardest:**
- **Extremely competitive** — Founding engineer roles attract top global talent
- **Visa sponsorship rare** — H-1B lottery, L-1 requires company sponsorship, O-1 needs extraordinary ability proof
- **Many companies won't hire international** — Even for remote roles
- **$1000/mo floor signals desperation** — US market rate for seniors is $150K+/year
- **Time zone mismatch** — 1-4 hours behind most of US

**Evidence from our search:**
- Many roles we found were: (a) too senior (Staff/Principal 10+ yr), (b) game dev (wrong fit), (c) Java-focused, (d) required clearance/citizenship
- High bounce rate on guessed emails

**Risk level:** HIGH — Low response rate expected, visa barrier is significant

## Recommendation: Focus Order

### Priority 1: BRASIL 🇧🇷
- **Action:** Double down on Brasil — more searches, more applications
- **Why:** Best language fit, no visa, same timezone, strong market
- **Expected outcome:** Highest interview conversion rate

### Priority 2: EU 🇪🇺
- **Action:** Target English-speaking roles in Germany, Netherlands, Portugal
- **Why:** High salaries, visa pathways exist, strong demand
- **Expected outcome:** Medium conversion, but highest payoff if successful

### Priority 3: LATAM 🌎
- **Action:** Apply to multinational companies with LATAM offices
- **Why:** Easy entry, good for building US/EU references
- **Expected outcome:** Good for getting first international reference

### Deprioritize: USA 🇺🇸
- **Action:** Only apply to exceptional matches (founding engineer at AI startup)
- **Why:** Too competitive for international seniors without US residency
- **Expected outcome:** Low response rate, don't waste energy
